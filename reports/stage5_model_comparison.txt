  pr_auc             area under the precision-recall curve (main metric, rare positive class)
  pr_auc_x_baseline  pr_auc divided by the fold's late rate (1.0 = no better than guessing)
  recall_top10       share of all late orders found inside the 10% of orders ranked riskiest
  precision_top10    share of those flagged orders that really were late
  lift_top10         precision_top10 divided by the fold's late rate
  f1_05              F1 at the default 0.5 cut-off (the real cut-off is chosen in Stage 6)
